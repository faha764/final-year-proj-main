What is Seller Protection in Craftify?

Currently, Craftify does not offer Seller Protection like other platforms. If an order is lost, delayed ,or damaged by seller and the customer faced any issue, and the issue is valid:

  • The customerr will receive a full refund.
  • But the seller will not receive any payment for that order.

When Does the Seller Lose Payment?

The seller is fully responsible for the safe and timely delivery of the product. In these situations, the seller will not receive payment:

  • Package is lost in transit.
  • If the order arrives after the estimated delivery date, payment may be withheld. However, if there is a valid reason for the delay, the payment will still be released.
  • Item is damaged during delivery.
  • Customer complains the item doesn't match description (and it's true).

Seller Responsibilities

To avoid disputes and payment loss, sellers must:

  • Ship items within the promised processing time.
  • Pack products safely to prevent damage.
  • Use reliable shipping services.
  • Add clear product descriptions and real images.
  • Always respond to customer messages or complaints.

What Happens During a Dispute?

  1. The customer will contact you first.
  2. If unresolved, the customer will complains to the admin through contact us page.
  3. Craftify team will review the case and order details.
  4. If the customer's complaint is valid:
    • The customer receives a refund.
    • The seller does not receive any earnings for that order.

How to Manage Your Shop During an Emergency

We understand that unexpected events like health issues, personal emergencies, or family responsibilities can affect your ability to run your shop. Here's how you can keep things on track even when life gets unpredictable.

1. Know Your Limits

  • How many orders can I manage right now?
  • Do I have the time and materials needed?
  • Will weather or local conditions delay shipping?

Be realistic and don't overcommit. It's okay to slow down temporarily.

2. Follow Craftify's Order Timelines

  • Delivery must be completed within 15 days after order confirmation.
  • Start processing only after admin verifies the payment and confirms order.
  • If you are unable to fulfill the order due to emergency, inform within 24 hours of order placement.

Shipping Policy (for Craftify Sellers)

Who Handles Shipping?

  • All products are shipped directly by the seller.
  • Sellers are responsible for safe, timely, and reliable delivery of orders.

Trusted Shipping Methods

  • Sellers must use trusted delivery services or courier partners.
  • Shipping method must ensure tracking and safety of the product.

Emergency Tracking

  • In case of delays or complaints, the Craftifys team may request tracking information from the seller.
  • Sellers must provide tracking or delivery proof if asked in emergencies.

Delivery Timeline

  • Orders must be delivered within 15 days of being confirmed.
  • This is mandatory unless a valid delay reason is communicated to the customer.
