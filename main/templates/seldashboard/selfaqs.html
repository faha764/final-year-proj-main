Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I add a new product?
Go to “List Product”, fill in all details like name, price, customizable details, category, and upload clear images. Submit to list it.
2. What is the “Customized” category option?
Select “Customized” if your product can be personalized (e.g. custom colors, sizes, etc.). Mention customization options in the description.
3. Where can I see all my listed products?
Go to “Manage Products” to view, edit, or delete your listings.
4. How do I know when someone is interested in my product?
You will receive a chat message if a customer wants to ask something.
5. How does the chat system work?
Customers can click the “Chat Now” button on your product page. You can see customer messages on your dashboard and answer of that question.
6. Can I see the customer's details before they place an order?
No, for privacy reasons, customer contact details are only shared once an order is placed.
7. What if I need to update my profile info?
Go to “Seller Profile” page and edit it by updating your name, profile photo, bio, or contact info. It will reflect in your public profile and product pages.
8. How do payments work on Craftify?
After the customer confirms delivery, the payment is processed and released to your account through admin.
9. How long does it take to receive payments?
Usually within 5-7 working days after order confirmation and delivery.
10. Can I cancel an order?
Yes, if there's a valid reason. You can contact to admin.
11. What if I receive a customization request I can't fulfill?
Politely reply in the chat explaining the issue or suggest alternatives. Don't accept the order if it's not possible.
12. Do I have to handle shipping?
Yes, currently sellers manage shipping. Provide delivery details clearly and use a reliable courier.
13. Is there a review or rating system?
Yes, customers can leave reviews after purchase. Good reviews help build trust and increase your visibility.
14. Where can I contact support if I face any problem?
You can message us through the Contact Us, or email at craftify966@gmail.com.
