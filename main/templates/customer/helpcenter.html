Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I place an order?
Select the product you like, customize if available, and click “Add to Cart”. Follow the checkout steps to place your order.
2. Can I customize products?
Yes, if the product is under the “Customized” category. You can discuss your preferences directly with the seller through the chat feature.
3. How can I contact the seller?
Click the “Chat” button on the product page to send them a message.
4. Where can I see my order history?
Go to your dashboard and click on “My Orders” to view all placed orders and their status.
5. How do I know my order is confirmed?
You will receive a confirmation notification that your order has been confirmed.
6. How can I track my delivery?
In the “My Orders” section, you can check current status of your order.
7. Can I cancel an order?
You can cancel your order from the “My Orders” page within 24 hours of placing it. After 24 hours, cancellation is not allowed.
8. When will I get my refund if I cancel?
Refund will be processed within 5-7 working days after cancellation confirmation.
9. How are payments made?
You can pay using available payment methods. The amount is held securely until the order is delivered.
10. Is my personal information safe?
Yes, Craftify values your privacy. Your contact details are only shared with the seller after you place an order.
11. What if I'm not satisfied with the product?
You can chat with the seller to discuss your concerns or raise an issue through our support team.
12. Can I rate or review a product?
Yes, after receiving your order, you can leave a review and rating on order page to help other customers.
13. How do I add a product to wishlist?
Click the "Add to Wishlist" on any product detail page to add it to your wishlist for future reference.
14. Can I exchange a product?
Non-customizable products can be exchanged, and only if there is a default or damage. You must request the exchange within 3 days after delivery by contacting support or the seller.
15. What should I do if my order is delayed?
If your order is delayed and the seller doesn't respond through the chat within a reasonable time, please contact the admin via the Contact Us page.
16. What happens if I don't respond after my order is delivered late?
If your delivery is delayed and you do not contact us within 3 days after the expected delivery date, Craftify will not be responsible for any claims related to that order.
17. When will I receive my order?
Orders are usually delivered within 15 days of confirming. If there is any delay or issue, please contact the seller through chat for updates.
18. How do I contact the admin?
You can reach out to the admin by visiting our Contact Uspage and submitting your query through the provided form.
