Exchange Policy

We understand that sometimes a product may not be right for you. That's why we offer easy exchange options but only for eligible products.

Which Products Can Be Exchanged?

  • Only non-customizable products are eligible for exchange.
  • Custom-made products are eligible for exchange only if customization option is not fulfiled.

How to Request an Exchange?

  • Contact the seller first and explain the issue.
  • If there is no response or an unfair refusal, then contact the admin to resolve the issue.

Valid Reasons for Exchange

You may request an exchange if:

  • You received the wrong product, size, or color.
  • The product is damaged or defective.
  • The product is not as described or shown.

Exchange Not Accepted If

  • You request an exchange after 3 days of delivery.
  • The product is used, washed, or damaged by you.
  • Original packaging, tags, or accessories are missing.

Conditions for Exchange

  • The product must be in original condition (unused, unwashed, undamaged).
  • It must include the original box, tags, and all accessories.
  • It must be securely packed for return.

Exchange Time Limit

  • You must request the exchange within 3 days of receiving the product.
  • Late requests will not be accepted.

Important Notes

  • Try to resolve the issue with the seller first.
  • The admin will step in, if no solution is provided.
  • Exchange depends on availability of the replacement item.
  • If the replacement product is out of stock, you can either choose a refund or select another product from the same seller as a replacement.
