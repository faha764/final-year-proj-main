How to Get Help with an Order

If you're facing an issue with your order, Craftify makes it easy to get help. Here's how:

Contact the Seller

The seller is the first and best person to assist you with your order. You can contact the seller directly through the “Product Details” page in the app.

Sellers can help you with:

  • Requesting returns or exchanges.
  • Any product-related questions.

Wait for the Seller to Respond

Please allow at least 48 hours for the seller to respond. Most sellers reply within 1-2 days.

If the Seller Doesn't Help

If your issue isn't resolved after 48 hours, you can:

  • Go to the Contact Us page in the Craftify app.
  • Fill in the form (include order ID and reason).

Our support team will review your complaint and respond accordingly.

What Happens Next?

For qualifying complaints (like non-delivery, damaged products, or wrong items), the Craftify team may issue a full refund after reviewing your case.

Note: Refund decisions are based on evidence, seller history, and platform policies.

Order Status

Status Options:

Each order will display a status, such as:

  • Pending: Order placed, waiting for admin confirmation.
  • Shipped: Seller has shipped the product.
  • Delivered: Product delivered to the customer.

Time Tracking:

  • 15 days countdown begins from the day the order is confirmed.
  • During this period, the customer can monitor the order status.

What If the Order Is NOT Delivered Within 15 Days?

If 15 days pass after confirming the order and the status is still not “Delivered”:

  • The customer should first contact the seller using the message option.
  • If the seller does not respond within 2 additional days.
  • The customer can then contact the Admin team for support or a refund.

How to Contact a Seller

Before Placing an Order

If a customer has a question about a product before purchasing:

  • Login or create an account.
  • Go to the Product Detail Page.
  • Click the “Chat Now” button.
  • Type your question and click Send.

The seller will respond you as soon as possible.

How Do I Change My Shipping Address?

For customers on the Craftify platform:

Quick Answer:

You can only change your shipping address before the seller ships your order.

To contact the seller, go to the Product Detail Page (not the Order Page).

If the Order Is Not Yet Shipped:

  • Visit the Product Detail Page.
  • Click “Chat Now”.
  • Send your correct shipping address.
  • If the order status is still "Confirmed", the seller can update your address.

If the Order Has Already Been Shipped:

  • The address cannot be changed after shipment.

How to Purchase an Item on Craftify

When you shop on Craftify, you're supporting local craftswomen and their handmade or customizable items. Here's how to place an order:

Step-by-Step: Placing an Order

1. Browse Products

  • Search or explore items from different sellers.
  • Click any item to visit the Product Detail Page.

2. Ask Questions (Optional)

  • Want to ask something before buying?
  • Use the "Chat Now" button on the product detail page to chat directly with the seller.

3. Add to Cart

  • Click "Add to Cart" on the product detail page.
  • Add more items if needed.

4. Cart

  • Go to your Cart.
  • Click "Proceed to Checkout".

5. Enter Details

  • Fill in your shipping address.
  • Click "Proceed to Payment".

6. Place Order

  • Review everything, select payment method, make payment and upload screenshot.
  • Click "Place Order".

7. Order Confirmation

  • You'll get a confirmation notification.
  • You can track your order from “My Orders” in dashboard.

When can a customer request a refund?

A customer can request a refund in the following cases:

  • The item did not arrive even after 15 days from the order date.
  • The admin or customer cancelled the order but the payment was already made.

Note: Refunds are processed only after approval from the seller or admin team.

When can a customer request a return or exchange?

A customer can request a return or exchange if:

  • The size, color, or design delivered was wrong compared to what was ordered.
  • The product is damaged or not functional.
  • The product is not customized as promised.

Conditions for Return & Exchange:

  • Request must be made within 3 days of product delivery.
  • Product should be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging.
  • Customer must upload photo proof (if damaged or wrong product received).
  • Custom or personalized items are not returnable, unless:
    1. Item is broken/damaged.
    2. Wrong item was sent.
  • Have more questions? Visit our Contact Us for support. We're happy to help!
